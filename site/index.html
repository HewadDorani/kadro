FAQ

A few things before you start.

Is Kandro free?+

The app is free to download and your first AI analysis and saved meal are free. After that you continue with Kandro Pro, with a 7-day free trial if eligible. Your saved meals, data controls and account deletion always stay available.

Do I need to register first?+

You can start with a guest account, without a password. During onboarding you set your goal and choose whether to consent to data processing. A verified email lets you restore your history on another device later.

How accurate is a meal photo?+

Photo analysis provides an estimate. Ingredients and portions can be misidentified, so you review and correct them before saving. Kandro shows confidence information and data sources.

Will it work on my phone?+

Kandro is available for iPhone with iOS 15.1 or later, in English and German. An Android version is not currently available.
